How this edition works

Adaptive questionsThe first question or reading set is average difficulty. Correct answers lead to harder questions and incorrect answers to easier ones.
No skipping, no going backEvery question needs an answer before you continue. Math and standalone English questions lock when you select Next.
Reading setsInside a reading passage set you can move among its questions. Answers lock when you select Submit set.
Question typesMultiple choice, answer entry, a drop-down, and a point-plotting question, like the technology-enhanced items on the real test. These items get no partial credit.
ToolsHighlighter, answer eliminator, notepad, zoom, and directions. Keep scrap paper handy for math.
ScoringYour score depends on how many you answer correctly and on how hard those questions were. You get a practice scaled score for each section and a composite.

Original practice material modeled on the SHSAT. It is not an official test and is not affiliated with the NYC Department of Education. The format follows published descriptions of the fall 2026 adaptive test; the real test uses a much larger question pool and its own scoring, so treat the scaled scores here as practice estimates. All questions also appear in the printable Form Q booklet.
